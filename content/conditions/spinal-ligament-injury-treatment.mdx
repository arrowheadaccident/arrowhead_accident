## Understanding spinal ligament injuries

Ligaments connect bones and help stabilize joints. A car accident can injure the ligaments that support the spine, causing pain and restricted movement.

- A **sprain** is an overstretched or torn ligament.
- A **strain** affects a muscle or tendon and may involve stretching or tearing.

These terms describe different tissues, so they are not interchangeable. An examination helps determine the location and severity of an injury.

## How sprains are graded

### Grade 1

A mild sprain involves stretching and minor damage to ligament fibers.

### Grade 2

A moderate sprain involves a partial tear. The affected joint may become less stable.

### Grade 3

A severe sprain involves a complete tear and can cause significant instability. Suspected spinal instability requires medical assessment before treatment.

## Recovery and treatment planning

Recovery depends on the location and severity of the injury and your overall health. A partial tear does not automatically mean permanent instability. We assess your symptoms and spinal function, and arrange further testing or a specialist referral when needed.
