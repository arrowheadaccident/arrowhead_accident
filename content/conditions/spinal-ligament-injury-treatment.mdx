## Did you suffer a spinal ligament injury?

After a car collision, the most common diagnosis for back pain in emergency medicine, and even in general medical practice, is a sprain/strain.

- A **sprain** is a micro or macro tear of ligament tissue.
- A **strain** is a micro or macro tear of muscle tissue.

Sprains and strains are graded by how much of the ligament or muscle is torn.

## How sprains are graded

The American Medical Association grades sprains by severity.

### Grade 1

The ligament is mildly damaged and slightly stretched, but it can still keep your spinal joints stable and allows normal function.

### Grade 2

The ligament has been stretched so far that it becomes too loose to hold the spinal joints together, causing a spinal dysfunction called spinal ligament laxity. This is a partial tear of the ligament, and the looseness it leaves can be permanent.

### Grade 3

A grade 3 sprain is a complete tear: the ligament has been split in two. This is the worst possible case for a spinal ligament, because it no longer gives any support to the spinal joint or joints it is attached to.

## Why ligament injuries heal slowly

The higher the grade, the worse the damage. But even a grade 1 sprain is serious, because ligament tissue has a very poor blood supply, found only where the ligament attaches to the bone. Ligaments simply can't repair and heal the way tissue with a rich blood supply, such as muscle, can. A grade 2 sprain, or partial tear, may never fully repair itself.

Your ligaments also contain special nerve endings, called mechanoreceptors, that tell the supporting muscles what is happening mechanically while you move. When a ligament has a grade 2 partial tear, it sends incorrect information to the surrounding muscles and spinal joints, which can lead to an ongoing spinal dysfunction called spinal ligament instability.

The AMA describes this dysfunction as "alteration of motion segment integrity." The definition of spinal instability is well established in the peer-reviewed medical literature and was described by White and Panjabi, leading experts in spinal biomechanics.

Spinal ligament injuries are among the most common causes of [neck](/neck-chiropractor-arrowhead-az/) and [back pain](/low-back-pain/) after car crashes, which is why every car collision patient deserves a careful exam for them.
