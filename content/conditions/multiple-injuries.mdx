## How physiotherapy helps

Car accident injuries can irritate the nerves and soft tissues of the head, neck and lower back and cause many different symptoms. Chiropractic physiotherapy, such as ultrasound, diathermy, massage therapy and exercise, can help these car and work injuries heal.

## Middle and lower back injuries

Car accidents can affect the middle and lower back as well as the neck. We take a detailed medical history and perform physical and orthopedic examinations to assess your symptoms. If a disc herniation is suspected, further tests or a specialist referral may be needed.
