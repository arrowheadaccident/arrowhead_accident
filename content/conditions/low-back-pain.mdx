## Experienced care for accident injuries

We have cared for car accident patients in Arrowhead, Glendale and the surrounding area for more than a decade.

Our practice focuses on car accident and work-related injuries. We help patients understand their symptoms and the treatment options available to them.

## How we examine your back after a car accident

If you have lower back pain or [neck pain](/neck-chiropractor-arrowhead-az/) after a car accident, call us for an examination. We will assess your symptoms and recommend care to support your recovery.

We begin with your medical history and an examination of your posture, movement and spinal function. Chiropractic and orthopedic tests help us assess possible injuries, such as a [spinal ligament sprain](/spinal-ligament-injury-treatment/). If a disc injury or another condition is suspected, imaging or a specialist referral may be needed. We then develop a treatment plan aimed at reducing pain and improving mobility.

Remember: a lack of pain after a car accident does not necessarily mean you are healthy and were not injured.
