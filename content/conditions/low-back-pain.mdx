## How we examine your back after a car accident

If you have a spinal injury such as low back or [neck pain](/neck-chiropractor-arrowhead-az/) after your car accident, we invite you to visit our clinic right away. We will make sure you receive the specific car accident treatment you need to fully recover.

Our car accident treatment protocol starts with a thorough examination of the posture of your neck, upper back and lower back to find any spinal injury or damage, such as a [spinal ligament sprain](/spinal-ligament-injury-treatment/). Chiropractic and orthopedic tests help us determine whether a disc is injured or herniated, and whether there are any undetected injuries from your accident. We then use specific chiropractic treatment protocols designed to reduce pain and improve mobility.

Remember: a lack of pain after a car accident does not necessarily mean you are healthy and were not injured.
