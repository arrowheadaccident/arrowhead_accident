## Why even a low-speed collision can hurt your neck

[Whiplash](/whiplash-injury/) is the primary cause of neck pain and damage after a crash. Even in a collision as slow as five miles per hour, your neck takes what is considered a violent, traumatic force. A minor rear-end collision typically sends the body in one direction and the head in another. This force can make the neck vertebrae collide and strain the muscles, tendons, [ligaments](/spinal-ligament-injury-treatment/) and blood vessels.
