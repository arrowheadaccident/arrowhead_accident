## Treating neck injuries and whiplash from auto accidents

Our chiropractors in Glendale, Arizona, assess and treat neck and upper back injuries after car accidents. These injuries can affect muscles, joints, ligaments and discs. We recommend care based on your symptoms and examination findings.

## Why even a low-speed collision can hurt your neck

[Whiplash](/whiplash-injury/) can occur when a collision suddenly moves the head and neck back and forth. Even a low-speed crash can strain the muscles and tendons or sprain the [ligaments](/spinal-ligament-injury-treatment/) of the neck. Vehicle damage alone does not determine whether you have an injury.

## A team of chiropractors, medical doctors and pain specialists

Our team brings together chiropractors, medical doctors and pain management specialists. We use your medical history and examination findings to plan treatment, with further testing or referrals when needed.

Neck pain can begin immediately after a collision or develop later. Not every crash causes whiplash, and pain alone does not identify the type or severity of an injury. An examination helps us determine what care is appropriate.

Tell your clinician about new or worsening symptoms, even if you initially felt well after the accident.
