## Symptoms and treatment of whiplash after a Glendale car accident

After a car accident, patients may experience [neck pain](/neck-chiropractor-arrowhead-az/), headaches and other symptoms of soft tissue injury. Whiplash is one possible cause. An examination helps determine what is causing your symptoms and which treatment is appropriate.

## What is whiplash?

Whiplash is a neck injury caused by a rapid back-and-forth movement of the head. It can affect the muscles, ligaments and other tissues of the neck. The type and severity of the injury vary from person to person.

A rear-end collision is a common cause, but whiplash can occur in other types of accidents too. Sudden acceleration or deceleration can move the head and neck beyond their usual range of motion.

## Symptoms of whiplash after a motor vehicle accident

Common symptoms include neck pain and stiffness, shoulder or upper back pain, headaches and dizziness. Some people also report fatigue, sleep problems or difficulty concentrating. Tell your clinician about all symptoms so that other injuries can be considered.

Symptoms may begin immediately or develop over the following days. Recovery time varies, and a reduction in pain does not always mean that recovery is complete. Your clinician can advise you on treatment, activity and follow-up care.

If you have neck pain after a car accident, arrange a medical assessment. Seek urgent care for severe or worsening symptoms.

### Headache

A headache can occur after a collision even without a direct blow to the head. It may be related to a neck injury, but a [head injury](/headaches-closed-head-injury/) is also possible. Seek medical assessment rather than waiting for the headache to pass.

### Soft tissue injury: sprains, strains, bumps and bruises

The soft tissues of the body are its muscles, ligaments, tendons, discs and nerves. Ligaments attach bones to bones, and tendons connect muscles to bones. A soft tissue injury is a bruise, an abnormal stretch or a tear of the soft tissue. A contusion is the medical term for a bruise. In medical terms, you strain a muscle or a tendon, but you sprain a ligament or a joint (see [spinal ligament injuries](/spinal-ligament-injury-treatment/)).

Soft tissue injuries may cause pain, tenderness, swelling, stiffness or reduced movement. Numbness or tingling can indicate nerve involvement and should be reported to your clinician. Recovery depends on the injury; some people need rehabilitation to regain strength and mobility.

## Please don't delay getting care after a car accident

Arrange an examination if you have pain or other symptoms after a crash. Emergency departments provide urgent assessment and treatment; ongoing symptoms may also need follow-up care. Follow any discharge instructions you receive.

Accurate, timely records help document your symptoms and treatment. If you have questions about insurance coverage or a claim, discuss your circumstances with your insurer or attorney.
