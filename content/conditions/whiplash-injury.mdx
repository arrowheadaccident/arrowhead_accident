## Is Whiplash a reality or a Myth?

Whiplash is not a formal medical term. It is a non-official medical description for an injury to the soft tissues (muscles, tendons, ligaments, nerves and discs) of the neck. Whiplash typically includes injuries to the joints between the neck vertebrae called facets, the disks in the neck, and the nerve roots that extend from the spinal cord in between the vertebra in your neck.

Whiplash which is caused by the sudden deceleration or rapid slowing of a vehicle in an accident. This instantaneous deceleration causes your head to “Whip” back and forth. The medical definition of what happens to your neck is that it is subjected to a sudden backwards and forward movement. Officially called extension- flexion injury.

### Soft Tissue Injury – Sprains, Strains, Bumps and Bruises oh my!

Medical terminology states, you strain a muscle or a tendon, but you sprain a [ligament](/spinal-ligament-injury-treatment/) or a joint.
