## No out-of-pocket cost when another driver is at fault

If another driver caused your car accident in the greater Phoenix area, our clinic offers treatment with no out-of-pocket cost to you. Call us to discuss your situation and how billing works.

Our doctors and staff work with your personal injury attorney and the insurance companies. We provide the treatment records and medical reports needed to document your injuries and support your claim.

## How billing works

- **We bill the at-fault driver's insurance.** If another person caused your accident, we bill their insurance and wait for payment.
- **Health insurance is not necessary.** You can be treated even if you don't have health insurance.
- **We work with your attorney.** If you have an attorney, we can defer payment under a lien until your case settles. A lien is an arrangement for payment from the proceeds of your claim; ask us about the terms.
- **We handle the paperwork.** Our staff prepare and submit the treatment records and billing documents.

Our office has over 10 years of experience caring for accident victims with no out-of-pocket costs.
