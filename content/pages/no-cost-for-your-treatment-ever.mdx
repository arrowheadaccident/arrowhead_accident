## We never ask the accident victim for payment

If you were in a car accident in the greater Phoenix area and were not at fault, you will have no out-of-pocket fees whatsoever. This applies when another driver is at fault; call us about your situation.

Our doctors and knowledgeable chiropractic staff work with your personal injury attorney and the insurance companies, and supply all the notes and medical reports they need to bring your case to a successful close, so you can get on with your life.

## How billing works

- **We bill the at-fault driver's insurance.** If another person caused your accident, we bill their insurance and wait for payment.
- **Health insurance is not necessary.** You can be treated even if you don't have health insurance.
- **We work with your attorney.** If you have an attorney, we work on a lien and wait for payment until your case settles.
- **We handle the paperwork.** All paperwork is handled for you.

Our office has over 10 years of experience caring for accident victims with no out-of-pocket costs.
