## Symptoms may not appear right away

After a car accident, symptoms of soft tissue injuries such as [whiplash](/whiplash-injury/) may take time to develop. An examination can help identify injuries and determine whether treatment or further testing is needed.

## Follow-up care after an ER visit

Emergency departments assess and treat urgent injuries and determine whether you can safely go home. Follow your discharge instructions, including any recommendations for follow-up care. Persistent pain may need further assessment even after an ER visit.

Our clinic offers same-day appointments for accident injuries on clinic days. For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to an emergency department.
