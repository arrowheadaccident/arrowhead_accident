## Hidden injuries can take days or weeks to show up

Getting examined and treated after a car accident is key, because collision forces often cause soft tissue injuries, such as [whiplash](/whiplash-injury/), that may not show symptoms until days or weeks after the accident. These are called hidden injuries.

## The ER looks only for life-threatening injuries

Emergency room doctors, by necessity, focus on life-threatening injuries after a car accident, and only on life-threatening injuries.

Once the ER doctor is satisfied that you don't have a life-threatening injury, you will be released, even if you have whiplash or other car accident symptoms. Usually you leave with a neck brace and common pain relievers, neither of which is meant to treat long-term accident damage.
