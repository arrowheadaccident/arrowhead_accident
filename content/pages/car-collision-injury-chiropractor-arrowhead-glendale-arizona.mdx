faqs:
  - q: "I've been to the ER and my doctor. Could I still need chiropractic care?"
    a: |
      You may need follow-up care if you still have neck pain, back pain or other symptoms. Emergency departments assess and treat urgent injuries, and your doctor can advise you on ongoing care. Follow any discharge instructions. Our clinic can assess whether chiropractic treatment is appropriate for your symptoms and coordinate referrals when needed.
  - q: "I haven't been to the ER or my doctor. Can I book an appointment right away?"
    a: |
      Yes. Call us to arrange an appointment, often the same or next clinic day, even if you have not yet seen another clinician. Our clinic provides outpatient injury care. For a medical emergency, call 911 or go to an emergency department.
  - q: "Should I see an attorney before a chiropractor?"
    a: |
      Do not delay needed medical care while arranging legal advice. An examination helps identify injuries and document the care you need. If you have questions about liability, insurance or a settlement, a personal injury attorney can advise you on your circumstances.
  - q: "What if my car sustained only minor damage?"
    a: |
      Minor vehicle damage does not rule out an injury. A clinical assessment considers your symptoms, medical history and examination findings, rather than vehicle damage alone. Arrange an examination if you have pain or other symptoms after a crash.
  - q: "What if I need an MRI or a medical specialist after my examination?"
    a: |
      We arrange imaging or a specialist referral when medically indicated. We work with providers who accept a lien, an arrangement that allows payment to be deferred until your case settles. Ask about the billing terms for each provider before receiving care.
  - q: "If I need chiropractic care, do I have to pay for it myself?"
    a: |
      When another driver is at fault, our clinic offers treatment with no out-of-pocket cost to you. We bill the at-fault driver's insurance and, when applicable, work with your attorney under a lien. Call us to discuss the arrangements for your situation.
  - q: "The insurance company wants to settle. What should I consider?"
    a: |
      Before agreeing to a settlement, make sure you understand your injuries, likely future care needs and what the agreement covers. A personal injury attorney can explain how a settlement may affect your ability to seek further payment. We can provide treatment records to help document your injuries.
  - q: "My insurance adjuster says a low-impact accident doesn't require chiropractic care. Is that true?"
    a: |
      The force of a collision and the visible damage to a vehicle do not, by themselves, determine whether you need treatment. A qualified clinician should assess your symptoms and recommend appropriate care. Some symptoms appear later, so report any new or worsening problems even if you initially felt well.
