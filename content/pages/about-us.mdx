## Injury chiropractors serving auto accident victims throughout Glendale

Have you or a loved one been injured in a car accident? Then you are in the right place. Our Glendale chiropractic auto injury clinic will give you the care and treatment you deserve to get back on your feet. When you call us, we act right away to get you the medical attention you need.

Our doctors have been treating auto accident victims for over 10 years, offering experienced care when you need it most. We limit our practice to auto accident and work-related injuries, so our staff are specifically qualified to handle your situation properly, from whiplash to other back injuries.

We seek payment only from the at-fault driver's insurance company, so you don't pay any out-of-pocket costs. When you come to us, you receive treatment right away, and we wait until your case has settled to be paid, so you can focus on recovering before thinking about any expenses.

Even if you don't have health insurance, you can still be treated for your car accident injuries. Your accident was stressful enough, and the last thing you need while you are hurting is a heap of paperwork and red tape. Our friendly, experienced staff will cut through it for you.

Don't wait for an explanation or a solution from the other driver's insurance company before you get medical attention. We can help you today.

## What to bring to your first visit

- The accident report, or the at-fault party's insurance information
- If you were injured at work, your workers' compensation information
