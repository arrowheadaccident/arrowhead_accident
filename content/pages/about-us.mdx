## Injury chiropractors serving auto accident victims throughout Glendale

Have you or a loved one been injured in a car accident? Our Glendale clinic provides chiropractic care for accident injuries and helps you take the next steps toward recovery. Call us to arrange an examination and discuss the care you need.

Our doctors have treated car accident patients for over 10 years. We focus on car accident and work-related injuries, from [whiplash](/whiplash-injury/) to [lower back injuries](/low-back-pain/). Our staff can help with both your care and the related paperwork.

When another driver is at fault, we seek payment from that driver's insurance company, with [no out-of-pocket cost to you](/no-cost-for-your-treatment-ever/). We provide treatment and wait for payment until your case settles, so you can focus on recovery. Call us to discuss the billing arrangements for your situation.

Even if you don't have health insurance, you can still be treated for your car accident injuries. Your accident was stressful enough, and the last thing you need while you are hurting is a heap of paperwork and red tape. Our friendly, experienced staff will cut through it for you.

Don't wait for an explanation or a solution from the other driver's insurance company before you get medical attention. We can help you today.

## What to bring to your first visit

- The accident report, or the at-fault party's insurance information
- If you were injured at work, your workers' compensation information
